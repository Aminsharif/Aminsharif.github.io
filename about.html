About

How I work

My approach to solving business problems

Start with the problem

Before choosing a tool, I want to understand the process, the people involved, and where time is actually being lost. Technology comes after that — never before.

Build the smallest reliable system

The best automation is usually the simplest one that solves the problem. I avoid unnecessary complexity that becomes hard to maintain later.

Assume things will fail

APIs go down and data is inconsistent. Error handling, retries and alerts are part of the build, not an afterthought bolted on later.

Leave you in control

Everything is delivered in your accounts with clear documentation and a handover, so you are never dependent on a black box you cannot inspect.

Continuous learning

Keeping skills current

The AI tooling landscape moves quickly, so I keep learning deliberately rather than chasing every new release.

Agent workflows

Completed training on building agentic workflows, which informs how I design AI steps that can use tools and make decisions safely.

Retrieval systems

Hands-on work with retrieval and knowledge systems — the layer that keeps AI support answers grounded in real, up-to-date content.

Automation at scale

Ongoing practice with n8n and API integration patterns, including the unglamorous reliability work that production systems depend on.
