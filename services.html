Services

AI automation services for growing businesses

I design, build and maintain automations that remove repetitive work, connect disconnected tools, and put AI to work where it genuinely helps. Every service below starts from a business problem.

Service 01

Workflow Automation with n8n

The business problem: your team spends hours copying data between applications, sending manual notifications, and keeping records in sync by hand.

I build n8n workflows that connect your applications so data moves automatically, reliably, and with an audit trail.

App-to-app connections Data synchronisation Automated notifications Scheduled jobs Conditional logic
View the n8n service

What you can expect

  • Applications connected through native nodes, APIs and webhooks
  • Repetitive tasks and manual notifications removed
  • Error handling, retries and alerts built in
  • Clear documentation and a handover walkthrough

Service 02

CRM Automation & Integrations

The business problem: leads arrive from many channels, sit unattended, and pipeline data goes stale because updates are manual.

I connect your forms, channels and tools to the CRM you already own, so capture, qualification and follow-up happen without someone remembering to do them.

Lead capture Qualification Assignment & routing Follow-ups Pipeline updates Reporting
View the CRM service

What you can expect

  • Every lead captured once and stored consistently
  • Leads scored and assigned without manual triage
  • Follow-ups scheduled reliably, stopping when a reply arrives
  • Cleaner pipeline data for reporting

Service 03

AI Voice Agents

The business problem: calls outside working hours go unanswered, and staff time is spent on repetitive scheduling and qualification calls.

I build voice workflows that answer inbound calls, handle common requests, book appointments against real availability, and route anything complex to a person.

Inbound inquiries Appointment scheduling Lead qualification Call routing CRM logging
View the voice agent service

What you can expect

  • Calls answered consistently, including outside hours
  • Appointments booked against real calendar availability
  • Qualification questions asked the same way every time
  • Every conversation logged to your CRM

Service 04

AI Customer Support Systems

The business problem: the same questions arrive every week, response times stretch, and customers repeat themselves across channels.

I build support workflows that answer from your own documentation, retrieve relevant knowledge, look up live data in your systems, and escalate complex cases to a human.

Answer from your docs Knowledge retrieval Order / account lookups Human escalation Multi-channel
View the support service

What you can expect

  • Answers grounded in your own documentation, not guesses
  • Immediate responses to common questions
  • Clear escalation when the system is not confident
  • A consistent experience across chat and messaging channels

Service 05

RAG & Knowledge Base AI

The business problem: answers live across scattered documents, and a general chatbot replies confidently without matching your real content.

I build retrieval-augmented knowledge systems that answer only from your own documents, search by keywords and meaning together, and cite the source of every answer.

Answers with citations Hybrid search Reranked results Document ingestion API for your tools
View the RAG service

What you can expect

  • Answers grounded in your own content, with the source shown
  • Keyword and semantic search combined, then reranked
  • A repeatable pipeline that keeps the index up to date
  • An API or widget that fits into the tools you already use
