Problems We Solve

The bottlenecks automation usually removes

Automation does not guarantee results on its own. It removes manual friction and delays — so the outcomes depend on the process and data behind it.

Manually entering leads into a CRM

Someone retypes form submissions and enquiries into the CRM, one by one, and details get missed.

Capture the lead once and create the CRM contact automatically, with the source attached.

Forgetting to follow up with prospects

Follow-ups depend on memory and reminder notes, so warm leads go cold quietly.

Schedule follow-ups that trigger on time and stop as soon as a reply arrives.

Responding slowly to customer inquiries

Enquiries wait in a shared inbox until someone is free, and the first impression slips away.

Send an immediate acknowledgement and route the message to the right owner.

Handling repetitive support questions

The same handful of questions is answered by hand, again and again, every week.

Answer from your own documentation and escalate anything the system is unsure about.

Manually scheduling appointments

Back-and-forth emails just to find a time that works for both sides.

Let people book against real availability, with confirmations and reminders handled for you.

Data trapped in disconnected tools

The CRM, the billing tool, and the support desk all hold a partial version of the truth.

Keep systems in sync through APIs and webhooks, with error handling when a step fails.

FAQ

Questions clients ask before starting

What types of business processes can you automate?
Lead capture and follow-up, CRM updates, appointment scheduling, internal notifications, document handling, reporting, and customer support responses — anything repetitive that moves data between tools or waits on a manual step. If a process follows a rule, it is usually a candidate.
Can you integrate with our existing CRM?
Yes. If your CRM has an API, webhooks, or is supported by n8n — that includes HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Zoho and others — it can usually be integrated. If a tool has no API at all, we can discuss database-level or custom alternatives during discovery.
Can you connect different business applications?
That is the core of most projects. I connect apps through native n8n nodes, REST APIs and webhooks so data flows automatically between them instead of being copied by hand.
Can you build AI voice agents?
Yes — voice workflows for inbound inquiries, appointment scheduling, lead qualification and call routing. They are built on established voice platforms and connected to your calendar and CRM so the conversation produces a real record.
How long does an automation project take?
Timelines depend on scope and how many systems are involved. A focused, single-workflow build is measured in days, while multi-system projects run longer. You get a clear estimate after the discovery call, once the requirements are known — I do not quote fixed timelines before then.
How do you test and secure automation workflows?
Workflows are tested against real scenarios, including failure cases, before launch. Error handling, retries and alerts are built in. Credentials are stored in the platform's secure store, access is limited to what each step needs, and no secrets are placed in frontend code.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance?
Yes. Monitoring, fixes and improvements are available after handover as an optional ongoing arrangement. You always receive documentation so you are not locked in.
How does pricing work?
Pricing is scoped per project based on complexity, the number of integrations, and the level of AI involved. After the discovery call you receive a clear proposal. I do not publish fixed prices because an honest number depends on what the project actually requires.
