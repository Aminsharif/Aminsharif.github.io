Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use aminsharif.com, why it is collected, and how it is handled. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Note: this is a general-purpose template provided with the website. It is not legal advice. Please have it reviewed and adapted to your specific jurisdiction and business before relying on it.

1. Who is responsible

This website is operated by Amin Sharif, an independent AI automation developer. For any privacy question you can write to sharifmia236@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

When you submit the project inquiry form, you choose to provide:

  • Your name and business email address
  • Your company name or website (optional)
  • The service you are interested in
  • A description of your business problem
  • An optional budget range and preferred contact method

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and, if we work together, to deliver the agreed service.

3. Information collected automatically

This website is deliberately lightweight. It does not run advertising trackers or sell data. Two technical details are worth stating:

  • Theme preference. Your light/dark choice is stored in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent to a server.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider may record standard technical information (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability. This is normal for any website.

4. Third-party services

A small number of trusted services are used to make the site work:

  • FormSubmit.co delivers the inquiry form to my email inbox. The details you enter are transmitted through this service.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome are loaded from content delivery networks to render typography and icons.
  • Chat widget. If you use the support chat, your messages are sent to the chat service endpoint so an answer can be generated. Do not enter sensitive personal or payment information in the chat.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not currently run third-party analytics. If privacy-friendly analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated and any consent requirements will be respected.

6. How long information is kept

Inquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of the conversation. You can ask for your details to be deleted at any time.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information held about you, or to object to its processing. To make a request, email sharifmia236@gmail.com.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are taken to protect information in transit and at rest. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or applicable law changes. The “last updated” date at the top will reflect any revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to sharifmia236@gmail.com or through the contact page.
