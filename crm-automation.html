The problem and how I solve it

What goes wrong

  • Leads arrive through several channels and get lost between them
  • Contacts are created by hand, and duplicates pile up
  • Follow-ups depend on someone remembering to check
  • Pipeline stages go stale, so forecasting is guesswork

What automation changes

  • Every lead captured once, from every channel, with its source attached
  • Duplicates detected before a record is created
  • Follow-ups scheduled automatically, and stopped once a reply arrives
  • Pipeline data kept clean enough to report on

How a CRM workflow is built

A lead arrives from a form, chat, email or call
The contact is checked for duplicates against existing records
The lead is scored and qualified against your criteria
The record is created and assigned to the right owner
Follow-ups and pipeline updates run on schedule

What is included

  • Multi-channel lead capture into one reliable flow
  • Duplicate detection and contact matching
  • Qualification rules, scoring and owner assignment
  • Automated follow-up sequences with reply detection
  • Pipeline stage updates and reporting data
  • Error handling, alerts and documentation

Your CRM stays the system of record. Automation enriches and moves data — it does not replace the tool your team knows.

Frequently asked questions

Which CRMs do you work with?
HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Zoho and any other CRM that exposes an API or webhooks. If your CRM is supported by n8n, integration is usually straightforward.
Do we have to switch CRM?
No. The goal is to work with the CRM you already own. Replacing a CRM is a far bigger decision than automating it, and it is usually unnecessary.
Will it work with our website forms and other channels?
Yes. Website forms, landing pages, chat, email and phone enquiries can all feed the same capture workflow, so leads arrive through one reliable path instead of several disconnected ones.
How do you avoid duplicate contacts?
Capture workflows check for existing records by email or a unique identifier before creating anything, and update the existing contact instead of adding a duplicate.
Is our customer data handled securely?
Access is limited to what each step needs, credentials are stored in the platform's secure store, and only the data required for the task is passed between systems. No secrets are ever placed in frontend code.
