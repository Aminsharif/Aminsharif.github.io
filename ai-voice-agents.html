Service

AI Voice Agents

Voice workflows that answer inbound calls, handle common requests, book appointments against real availability, qualify leads, and route anything complex to a person.

Inbound inquiries Appointment scheduling Lead qualification Call routing CRM logging
Book a Discovery Call See a booking demo

The problem and how I solve it

What goes wrong

  • Calls outside working hours ring out and the enquiry is lost
  • Staff spend their day on routine scheduling and repeat questions
  • Qualification questions are asked inconsistently, so leads arrive unqualified
  • Details from a call are never written down properly

What automation changes

  • Calls answered consistently, whenever they arrive
  • Appointments booked against real calendar availability
  • The same qualification questions asked every time, in the same order
  • Every conversation logged to your CRM with a summary

How a voice workflow is built

A call comes in and the agent greets the caller clearly as an AI assistant
It understands the request and asks the qualifying questions you define
For bookings it checks real availability and confirms a time
The outcome is written back to your CRM and calendar
Anything outside scope is transferred to a human with full context

What is included

  • Call flow design around your real scripts and policies
  • Knowledge and guardrails so the agent stays on topic
  • Calendar and CRM integration for bookings and logging
  • Human handover rules and escalation paths
  • Testing across realistic scenarios before go-live
  • Documentation and guidance for your team

Honest expectation setting: a voice agent handles a defined scope well — it is not a replacement for every human conversation. We agree the scope up front and design clear handovers for everything else.

Frequently asked questions

Do callers know they are speaking to an AI?
Yes, and they should. The agent is introduced clearly at the start of the call. Being upfront is both more honest and more effective than pretending otherwise.
What happens if the agent cannot help?
Calls are routed to a human when the agent is unsure, when the caller asks for a person, or when the request falls outside the defined scope. The handover includes the context already gathered, so the caller does not have to start over.
Can the agent book against real availability?
Yes. The agent checks your calendar, offers times that are actually free, books the appointment, and sends a confirmation — then writes the booking back to your CRM.
Which voice platforms do you build on?
Established AI voice platforms combined with telephony providers and orchestrated through n8n and your business systems. The exact stack is chosen based on your call volume, language and integration needs.
Is call recording handled properly?
Recording and consent requirements differ by country and region. We discuss your jurisdiction during discovery and build the appropriate notice and consent step into the call flow rather than assuming.
