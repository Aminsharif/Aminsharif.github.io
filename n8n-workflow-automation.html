Workflow Automation with n8n

Connect your applications, eliminate repetitive tasks, automate notifications, and keep business data synchronised across systems — with workflows built to keep running.

The problem and how I solve it

What goes wrong

  • The same data is typed into two or three different tools
  • Notifications are sent by hand, and sometimes forgotten
  • Records drift out of sync, so nobody fully trusts the numbers
  • Time is spent on work a computer could do reliably

What automation changes

  • One workflow connects the apps that used to be separate
  • Notifications and hand-offs fire automatically and on time
  • A single source of truth is kept in sync as things change
  • Your team spends time on judgement, not data entry

How an n8n workflow is built

A trigger starts the workflow — a new record, a form, a schedule or an incoming webhook
Data is validated, cleaned and shaped into a predictable format
Conditional logic decides what should happen next, case by case
Connected apps are updated through their APIs and webhooks
Failures are caught, retried and alerted instead of failing silently

What is included

  • A scoped workflow outline agreed before development
  • Connections built with native nodes, REST APIs and webhooks
  • Validation, transformation and duplicate protection
  • Error handling, retries and alerting on failure
  • Testing against real scenarios and edge cases
  • Documentation and a handover walkthrough

Workflows are delivered in your n8n instance and account. You keep ownership and full visibility of what runs.

Frequently asked questions

What is n8n and why build on it?
n8n is a workflow automation platform you can self-host or run in the cloud. It connects hundreds of apps and also lets us write custom code when a service has an unusual API. That flexibility is why it suits real business processes, which rarely fit neatly into a fixed template.
Which applications can you connect?
Any service with a REST API, webhook or native n8n node — CRMs, email, Google Workspace, databases, payment tools, form tools and AI providers. If a tool has no API at all, we discuss database-level or custom alternatives during discovery.
Do I need to host n8n myself?
Not necessarily. We can use n8n Cloud or a self-hosted instance depending on your data, budget and compliance needs. I will recommend the option that fits your situation rather than the one that is easiest for me.
How do you stop workflows from failing silently?
Every production workflow includes error handling, retries and alerting. When a step fails, the failure is captured and reported rather than disappearing, and important steps are made idempotent so a retry cannot duplicate work.
Can you take over or fix an existing n8n setup?
Yes. I regularly review existing workflows, identify fragile or failing steps, and either stabilise them or rebuild the parts that are not worth maintaining.
