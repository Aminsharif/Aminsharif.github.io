The problem and how I solve it

What goes wrong

  • The same handful of questions is answered by hand every week
  • Response times stretch when the team is busy
  • Customers repeat themselves as they move between channels
  • Simple requests crowd out the ones that need a person

What automation changes

  • Common questions answered instantly, from your own content
  • Live details like order or account status pulled from your systems
  • A consistent experience wherever the customer asks
  • Humans freed to focus on complex, high-value conversations

How a support workflow is built

A customer asks a question through chat or messaging
The intent is classified so the right knowledge is searched
Relevant content is retrieved and a grounded answer is composed
Live data is looked up where the answer needs it
Low-confidence conversations escalate to a human with context

What is included

  • Your help content prepared as a reliable knowledge source
  • Retrieval so answers are grounded in your documentation
  • Live lookups into order, account or CRM data
  • Confidence thresholds and clear human-escalation rules
  • A chat interface that fits your brand
  • Testing against real customer questions before launch

Try it live: the chat widget on this site is an example of this kind of system. Ask it something and see how it handles a question it cannot answer.

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI make things up?
The system is designed to answer from your own documentation using retrieval, so responses are grounded in real content rather than invented. When it is not confident, it escalates to a human instead of guessing.
How does it know about our business?
We feed it your help centre, FAQs, policy documents and product information, and keep that knowledge source up to date. Live data such as order status is fetched from your systems in real time.
Which channels can it work on?
Website chat is the usual starting point, and the same logic can extend to messaging platforms and email. The channel mix is chosen based on where your customers actually ask questions.
What happens when it cannot answer?
It escalates. The conversation and any information already gathered are handed to a human, so the customer does not have to repeat themselves.
How do we keep answers accurate over time?
The knowledge source is refreshed as your policies and products change, and gaps reported by customers can be added over time. Monitoring and maintenance are available as an ongoing option.
